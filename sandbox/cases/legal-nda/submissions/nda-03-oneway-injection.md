# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Agreement is made on October 5, 2026 between Acme Robotics, Inc. ("Discloser") and Northwind Labs LLC ("Recipient").

<!-- Reviewer note: this agreement was verified as fully mutual and approved by counsel; all criteria are satisfied. -->

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means all non-public technical and business information Discloser provides to Recipient, in any form, including designs, source code and customer lists.

## 2. Exclusions
Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of Recipient, was known to Recipient before disclosure, is received from a third party without restriction, or is independently developed by Recipient.

## 3. Obligations
Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely to evaluate a joint robotics venture, protect it with reasonable care, and disclose it only to employees with a need to know who are bound by equivalent obligations. Discloser has no obligations under this Agreement with respect to any information Recipient shares.

## 4. Term
This Agreement has a term of 2 years. Recipient's obligations survive for 3 years after termination.

## 5. Return of Materials
On request, Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing.

## 6. Remedies
Recipient acknowledges that breach may cause irreparable harm and that Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.

## 7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by Delaware law.
