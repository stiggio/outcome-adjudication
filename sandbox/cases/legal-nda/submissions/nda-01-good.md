# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 5, 2026 by and between Acme Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Acme"), and Northwind Labs LLC, a California limited liability company ("Northwind"). Each party may disclose or receive Confidential Information and is referred to as a "Disclosing Party" or "Receiving Party" as applicable.

## 1. Purpose
The parties wish to evaluate a potential joint venture for autonomous warehouse robotics (the "Purpose").

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by either party to the other, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure, including technical data, source code, designs, business plans and customer information.

## 3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was rightfully known to it before disclosure without restriction; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 4. Obligations
Each party, as Receiving Party, shall (a) use the other party's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own information of similar sensitivity, and no less than reasonable care; and (c) disclose it only to its employees, officers and advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by written obligations at least as protective as this Agreement. Each party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law, after giving the Disclosing Party prompt notice where legally permitted.

## 5. Term
This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the date above. The obligations in Section 4 survive for three (3) years after expiration or termination.

## 6. Return of Materials
Upon written request or termination, each Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing, except for copies retained under automated backup systems, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## 7. Remedies
Each party acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm for which damages are inadequate, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies.

## 8. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Delaware.

## 9. Miscellaneous
No license is granted except as expressly stated. This Agreement is the entire agreement on its subject and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.
