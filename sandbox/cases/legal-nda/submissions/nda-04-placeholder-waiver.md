# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Agreement is between Acme Robotics, Inc. and [COUNTERPARTY NAME], each a "Party".

## Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means non-public information either Party discloses to the other that is marked or reasonably understood as confidential.

## Exclusions
Information that is public, previously known, received from a third party without restriction, or independently developed is excluded.

## Obligations
Each Party shall use the other's Confidential Information only to evaluate the venture, protect it with reasonable care, and limit disclosure to personnel with a need to know.

## Term
Term: 2 years. Confidentiality obligations survive 3 years after termination.

## Return of Materials
Each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information on request.

## Remedies
Each Party waives any right to seek injunctive or equitable relief and agrees that monetary damages are its sole remedy for breach.

## Governing Law
Delaware law governs. Northwind Labs LLC is referenced for notice purposes only.
